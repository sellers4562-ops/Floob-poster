Privacy Policy

FLOOB Poster · effective October 24, 2026 · home

FLOOB Poster only ever connects to FloobMasta's own YouTube, TikTok and Twitch accounts. It collects no data from anyone else.

  • Google user data it uses: permission to upload videos to FloobMasta's YouTube channel (youtube.upload), read the channel's basic info and video statistics (youtube.readonly), and read the channel's YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly) for FloobMasta's own growth dashboard.
  • How it's used: to publish videos FloobMasta approves and to show him his own channel numbers. Nothing else.
  • Where it's stored: sign-in tokens and statistics stay on FloobMasta's computer only, in a folder readable by his user account. There is no server or database.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. FLOOB Poster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removal: revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the tool's credentials folder removes all stored tokens.
