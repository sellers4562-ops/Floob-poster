FLOOB Poster

A personal posting tool run by the Twitch streamer FloobMasta.

FLOOB Poster is a private script on FloobMasta's own computer. It takes highlight reels cut from FloobMasta's Twitch streams and uploads them to FloobMasta's own YouTube channel as Shorts, or sends them to FloobMasta's own TikTok inbox. Every post is approved by FloobMasta first. It is not offered to anyone else.
